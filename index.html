It is the season, friends, when good people everywhere put down their work, put on their finest, and gather where the lamps are lit and the table is full. Once again Moorhead Bros. will keep open house—and you, dear reader, are most particularly wanted.

There will be eggnog in quantity and cookies beyond all reason. For every Moorhead employee there will be a handsome new sweater, in the proper size, with our compliments. And we have it on excellent authority that a certain stout gentleman from the North intends to look in.

Bring your wife, your husband, or your sweetheart. It costs you nothing but a moment: simply fill in the coupon below and send it off to-day!
